## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies **Bright Editorial School Life**—a dynamic fusion of authentic bilingual campus vigor, institutional trust, and human-first storytelling. It captures the dual perspective of prospective parents seeking academic excellence and stability, alongside spirited students craving creativity, community, and active discovery. 

The aesthetic pivots away from synthetic, tech-saturated tropes (no generic purple-blue gradients, floating blobs, or sterile glassy surfaces). Instead, it draws inspiration from premium physical yearbooks, modern international broadsheets, and high-energy collegiate magazines:
- **Architectural Clarity & Editorial Stride:** Crisp 1px rule lines, structured indices, monospaced metadata stamps, and prominent numbered section signifiers (`01`, `02`, `03`) anchor the institutional rigor.
- **Human Warmth & Kinetic Cadence:** Asymmetric compositions, varied photographic crops (tight candid portraits, expansive athletic panoramas, and intimate classroom macro shots), and confident editorial typography communicate an open, warm, and thriving educational ecosystem.
- **Intentional Density:** Breathing room is purposeful and unbloated, allowing decisive typography and real photography to command attention and drive seamless admission conversions.

## Colors

The palette adheres strictly to a calculated 60-25-15 architectural weight, engineered to communicate heritage credibility alongside vivid school vitality:

- **60% Light Architectural Neutrals:** 
  - Canvas Base: Warm off-white (`#FAF9F5`) to prevent optical glare and evoke premium uncoated editorial paper.
  - Surface Pure: Bright natural white (`#FFFFFF`) reserved for cards, focal panels, and active input fields.
  - Hairlines & Rulers: Warm hairline borders (`#E5E2DC`) providing structural definition without heavy dark outlines.
  - Ink & Body Charcoal: Subdued deep slate (`#1C2630`) and neutral caption grey (`#5A6472`) for continuous reading comfort.

- **25% Signature Heritage & Energy:**
  - Royal Navy (`#0E2A47`): The institutional backbone. Delivers commanding authority across primary buttons, massive headlines, navigational structures, and editorial badges.
  - Solar Amber Gold (`#E58A00` with energetic highlight `#F59E0B`): Radiates scholastic warmth, optimism, and prestige. Employed for active conversion triggers, bilingual program callouts, and key highlight rules.

- **15% Expressive Campus Accents:**
  - Dynamic Terracotta / Coral (`#E05338`): Infuses spontaneous life into open-house alerts, upcoming registration deadlines, and badge accents.
  - Collegiate Forest Green (`#1E6B52`): Denotes athletic spirit, environmental sustainability, and holistic well-being; leveraged across campus facility spotlights, extracurricular tags, and confirmed admission state indicators.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured editorial verve of **Epilogue** with the humane, balanced clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:

- **Epilogue (Headlines & Markers):** Provides strong graphic impact, rhythmic counter-spaces, and a collegiate editorial atmosphere. It is authoritative without feeling stiff or archaic. Section numerals (e.g., `01 / CURRICULUM`) and key punchy statements rely on tight negative tracking to maintain punch.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Utility, & Forms):** Handles bilingual text switching (Indonesian and English) with consistent optical x-height and clear distinction between similar glyphs. Its open apertures ensure legibility across admission forms, fee schedules, and academic narratives.
- **Typographic Rules:**
  - Section headers must incorporate upper-case metadata breadcrumbs set in `label-sm` above the main `headline-xl`.
  - Body copy retains a 150-160% line height ratio to guarantee high readability for parents reviewing long-form academic curricula.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are orchestrated using an **asymmetric 12-column editorial grid** on desktop, shifting to a 6-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column structured stack on mobile:

- **Asymmetry & Photo Cadence:** Rather than repetitive uniform cards, layouts break into intentional pairings: an 8-column wide landscape photo paired against a 4-column rich textual insight; or an alternating mosaic with a tall 5-column portrait crop offset beside a 7-column admission highlight panel.
- **Editorial Line Dividers:** Sections are partitioned by explicit 1px rules (`#E5E2DC`). Margin gutters are preserved to allow editorial annotations and registration step numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`) to hang in the left gutter space on viewports above 1280px.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** Full 12-column layout with fixed outer margins (`margin`: `3rem`), section gaps leveraging `space-3xl` (`6rem`), and asymmetric multi-tier columns.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 6-column layout with condensed gutters (`1.25rem`) and unified `space-2xl` section spacing.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Linear 4-column layout; gutter drops to `1rem` and canvas margins tighten to `1.25rem`. Photo collages convert into horizontally swipeable, snap-aligned filmstrips with explicit edge margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial dropshadows and fuzzy blurs, utilizing **tactile physical layering and low-contrast borders**:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: Warm Sand (`#FAF9F5`).
  - Elevated Cards / Modals: Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid border (`#E5E2DC`).
  - Active / Focus Layer: High-contrast Navy frame (`#0E2A47`) or 2px Solar Gold focus rings (`#E58A00`).
- **Tactile Shadows (Strictly Constrained):** Floating elevation is almost entirely replaced by structural line weights. Where interactive elevation is necessary (such as sticky conversion bars and active drag-and-drop file upload fields), use a tight, crisp, tinted offset shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(14, 42, 71, 0.07)`.
- **Editorial Framing:** Photographs employ a 1px inner or outer hairline bounding border (`#E5E2DC`), evoking printed magazine plates and preserving contrast against the creamy background canvas.

## Shapes

The shape system employs disciplined, moderate corner geometry (`roundedness: 2`, where base components feature `0.5rem` / 8px radius, and cards scale to `1rem` / 16px radius). This avoids the infantile appearance of hyper-rounded bubbles while softening standard institutional rigidity:

- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Selects):** Standardized to 8px (`0.5rem`) for a tailored, crisp, and tactile feel.
- **Card Containers & Admission Lite Drawers:** Set to 12px or 16px (`1rem`), harmonizing comfortably with editorial imagery.
- **Editorial Images:** Can alternate between clean 0px corners (when full-bleed or paired with a framed rule) and 12px corner rounding when placed as floating inset figures.
- **Pill Tags & Category Badges:** Strictly reserved for status pills and category indicators (e.g., "Open Enrollment", "Bilingual Cambridge & Merdeka").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Signature Royal):** Solid `#0E2A47` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px border radius, font `label-lg`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#12335C` with a micro-shift (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary (Solar Spark):** Energetic `#E58A00` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px border radius. Used primarily for immediate conversion triggers like "Daftar PSB Sekarang / Apply Now".
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1.5px solid `#0E2A47`, text `#0E2A47`. Hover triggers `#FAF9F5` fill.
- **Text Action:** Epilogue font with an accompanying directional arrow (`→`), underlined by a crisp 1.5px rule with a 4px gap.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Category Badges:** Compact pill tags (`padding: 4px 10px`), upper-case `label-sm`. 
- **Variants:**
  - *Academic / Bilingual:* Navy tint (`#0E2A47` background, white text).
  - *Admissions Status:* Forest green tint (`#E8F3EE` background, `#1E6B52` text).
  - *Alert / Urgent Intake:* Coral tint (`#FBECE8` background, `#E05338` text).

### Form Inputs & Admission Lite Stepper
- **Fields:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border (`#E5E2DC`), 8px border radius, and `0.75rem 1rem` padding. Inactive placeholders rest in neutral gray (`#5A6472`).
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border in `#0E2A47` with a zero-offset outline ring in solar gold (`#E58A00` at 30% alpha).
- **Admission Stepper:** Minimal numbered horizontal tracks (`01 Data Siswa`, `02 Pilih Jalur`, `03 Konfirmasi`) connected by 1px rules; completed stages turn `#1E6B52` with checkmarks; active stages highlight in `#0E2A47`.

### Cards & Editorial Modules
- **Variable Photo-Story Cards:** Never repeat identical three-card column formats. Employ heterogeneous layouts:
  - *Hero Testimonial Card:* 60% parent quote in `headline-md`, 40% authentic photo portrait with school location stamp.
  - *Program Spotlight:* White canvas container, top photographic bleed with 12px top radius, bottom content panel featuring a bilingual badge, title, concise description, and an inline link.
- **Borders:** All cards carry a uniform 1px solid border (`#E5E2DC`) with no heavy blurred shadows.

### Interactive Selection (Checkboxes, Radios & Switches)
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 1.5px border `#0E2A47` with 4px border radius for checkboxes and full circle for radios. Selected state fills with `#0E2A47` and features an interior white mark.
- **Grade-Level Selector Tiles:** Interactive pressable rectangular tiles (e.g., "KB / TK", "SD", "SMP", "SMA") displaying program tags, localized fee cues, and distinct active border states.
